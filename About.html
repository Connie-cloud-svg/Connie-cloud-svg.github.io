About me

Hey, I'm Connie 👋

I'm Concepter Bosibori, a web developer based in Nairobi, Kenya I basically fell in love with building websites and haven't looked back since.

I'm a graduate of IYF Weekend Academy, where I built my foundation in HTML, CSS, and JavaScript before levelling up into React — learning by shipping real, component-driven apps. Since then I've expanded into full-stack territory: building REST APIs with Node.js and Express, working with MongoDB for data, and deploying projects live on Render and Vercel. I'm big on the idea that a good website has personality, not just clean code.

Outside coding, you'll find me out exploring, skating with friends, going for a bike ride, or chasing that perfect lighting for photos. Basically, I'm just a creative girly who codes.
